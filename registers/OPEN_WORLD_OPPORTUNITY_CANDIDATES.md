## Hard-gate rule

No candidate may be promoted to ENTRY-CANDIDATE or TEST-READY without:
1. observed problem or a verified procurement need;
2. material consequence/value;
3. named plausible legal-entity payer and buying authority;
4. current alternatives/internalization understood;
5. legal scope and credential boundary;
6. bounded deliverable and objective acceptance;
7. first delivery without speculative platform build;
8. manageable liability and working capital;
9. reachable buyer/channel;
10. credible direct payment test.

## Prior kills preserved

The following generic categories remain killed from prior research and are not reopened by this wave: generic procurement outsourcing, invoicing/collection outsourcing, maintenance/HSE/technical assistance, spare-parts sourcing, export paperwork, tender preparation, software/dashboard/automation, energy audits outside approved-auditor scope, and licensed waste-management operations. Any reopening requires a materially changed assumption and a named payer.
