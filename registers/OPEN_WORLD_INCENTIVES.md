### No false-maxima rules

- AWS: use the current detailed guide's $100K Portfolio ceiling; do not repeat older $200K headlines without a current offer document.
- Microsoft: $150K is a staged maximum; it requires real sustained Azure usage and service adoption. A partner-backed route can change the maximum to $200K.
- Google: $2K is the pre-funded Start tier; $200K general / $350K AI are Scale-tier benefits and require qualified funding stage and AI-first status where relevant.
- Cloudflare: $350K is the top tier, not the normal bootstrapped award; Tier 3 is $10K and general eligibility wording is internally ambiguous.
- Vercel: $30K is a partner-dependent flexible commitment, not a cash grant; one-year terms and a payment card apply.
- DigitalOcean: no fixed universal credit amount is published; actual awards vary and GPU/third-party model inference are excluded from core credits.
- NVIDIA Inception: membership is valuable ecosystem access, not a guaranteed cash/credit award.
- ASF: DZD 150M is an upper ticket advertised for equity/quasi-equity investment, not grant money or an automatic entitlement.
