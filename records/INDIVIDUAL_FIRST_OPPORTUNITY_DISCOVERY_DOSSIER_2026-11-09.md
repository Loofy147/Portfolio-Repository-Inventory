## 1. Executive decision

The objective is to combine **small, independently accessible opportunities** into a larger base of cash, useful resources, proof, access and reusable capability. "Small" describes the entry size and exposure—not the ambition or eventual scale.

The applicant/delivery unit is currently an **individual**. Do not make company formation, startup status, venture funding or accelerator acceptance a prerequisite. Individual fellowships, independent grants, open-source funding, paid technical tasks, bounties, challenge prizes, research work and individual service contracts are admissible opportunity forms.

The search remains open to any sector, geography, technology, market or transaction structure. No existing product or repository determines what may be discovered. Portfolio assets are considered only after an opportunity independently survives market and entry screening.

**Current evidence-based state:**
- No market candidate from the prior economic discovery wave passed every buyer, payment, legal, delivery and capital gate.
- Several individual funding and resource-access routes are documented below. They are **opportunities to check**, not approved money or guaranteed access.
- No active paid test, application, bid, purchase, contact, contract or account change was initiated.
- The next unresolved issue with the highest systemic impact is the ability to receive and classify different forms of foreign-origin funding/income lawfully from Algeria.

## 2. Strategic model: compound distinct value vectors

Track five vectors separately. They are complementary, but are not interchangeable.

| Vector | What counts | What does not count |
|---|---|---|
| Cash | Net payable amount, currency, fees, taxes, payment date, acceptance and probability | Advertised maximums, unapproved awards, hypothetical sales |
| Avoided cash cost | A free/subsidised resource that replaces a real planned purchase | Unused quota, inflated retail value, credits that cannot be consumed |
| Proof and reputation | Accepted work, merged upstream changes, verified results, public artifacts, references | Profile views, attendance, unverified claims, vanity metrics |
| Distribution and access | Actual buyer access, maintainer relationships, data rights, permitted channels, accepted introductions | Event attendance or network membership alone |
| Reusable capability | Tested components, operating methods, domain knowledge, credentials and lawful payment rails | Unmaintained code or a tool with no demonstrated use |

The compounding mechanism is conditional, not automatic:

1. A small paid task or award creates net cash and/or an accepted artifact.
2. Resource access reduces the cost of a specific next workload.
3. The artifact and delivery evidence improve access to another buyer, funder or maintainer.
4. Reusable methods reduce the cost and time of later delivery.
5. The next opportunity must still pass its own eligibility, payment, legal and acceptance tests.

Do not stitch unrelated grants into one misleading proposal. Each funder receives an honest, independently deliverable scope. Respect its IP, public-release, privacy, publicity, budget and reporting terms.

## 3. Search scope and evidence discipline

### 3.1 Demand-led opportunity anchor

For customer-funded work, use:

**Observable economic problem/event → consequence → current handling → gap/exception → affected owner → buyer/payer → bounded intervention → acceptance/payment test.**

Search for operational loss, rejection, delays, rework, evidence gaps, supplier mismatch, failed handoffs, disputes, regulatory changes, asset underperformance, inaccessible data, unused capacity, poor decisions and other observable economic events.

### 3.2 Opportunity classes

Search independently across:
- products, physical goods, software, hardware and manufacturing;
- services, maintenance, implementation, analysis, testing, accessibility, security, research and documentation;
- data, APIs, licensing, IP, standards and information access;
- sourcing, procurement, subcontracting, trade, logistics and distribution;
- under-used assets, capacity, inventory, skills, compute and infrastructure;
- grants, fellowships, prizes, bounties, research access, competitions and open-source funding;
- partnerships, channels, technology transfer, licensing and commercialization;
- local, regional and international markets, subject to actual access and lawful execution.

A grant, trend, public procurement notice, regulation, vendor absence or market-size forecast does not prove customer demand. Enablers are assessed separately from customer-funded opportunities.

### 3.3 Claim labels

Use **ESTABLISHED**, **EXPERIMENTALLY_SUPPORTED**, **LOCAL_MARKET_SIGNAL**, **USER_REPORTED**, **INFERENCE**, **HYPOTHESIS**, **CONTRADICTED**, **UNKNOWN**, **OPEN**, **KILLED** and **DEFERRED**. Every material claim must identify its source, publication/observation date, checked date, scope, what it establishes, what it does not establish, contradictory evidence and the next discriminating test.

Never promote a candidate to an executable entry opportunity without a real problem, material consequence, plausible payer and purchase authority, incumbent/workaround review, legal role, bounded deliverable, objective acceptance, reachable buyer, manageable capital/liability and credible payment route.

## 6. Algeria: legal and payment architecture

This is a critical feasibility track. Different incoming payment categories must not be treated as interchangeable.

### 6.1 Transaction classes to keep separate

1. **Grant/fellowship/stipend:** programme funding; determine the legal, bank and tax classification before acceptance.
2. **Prize/competition award/bounty:** governed by the award's rules, IP terms, KYC and payout mechanism; classification may differ from ordinary service revenue.
3. **Service-export income:** payment by a foreign customer for software, testing, research, design, documentation, consulting or another service delivered from Algeria.
4. **Resource credit:** in-kind service access; not cash income, and governed by provider terms.
5. **Investment, loan, reimbursement or cost recovery:** different legal and accounting character; never misdescribe one category as another.

### 6.2 Bank of Algeria Instruction 06-2021

**ESTABLISHED — official instruction, subject to current consolidated law and bank implementation**
- Instruction 06-2021 provides for a foreign-currency professional account for non-traders (compte devise professionnel non commerçant).
- Article 2 allows a resident natural or legal person carrying on lawful economic activity to open the relevant account(s).
- Article 4 addresses crediting export receipts for goods/services where repatriated within the applicable regulatory deadlines.
- Article 12 ties use of the professional non-merchant account to activity-related expenses and electronic payments.

**BOUNDARY:** this is not a general personal FX wallet for arbitrary transfers. Lawful activity, correct account classification, bank documentation, declarations and the applicable repatriation rules still matter.

### 6.3 Regulation 26-02 of 23 July 2026

Bank of Algeria Regulation 26-02 amended Regulation 07-01 on external payments and repatriation. Do not apply old deadlines or generic exporter assumptions without checking the current consolidated regulation and written bank instructions. The service-export route must not automatically be extended to grants, prizes or stipends.

### 6.4 ANAE / auto-entrepreneur status

ANAE's official activity catalogue includes eligible digital services, consulting/expertise/training and business-service activities. Auto-entrepreneur status may be an optional legal/invoicing route for an individual whose actual activity and profile fit the rules.

It is not a universal solution and is not a startup identity. Verify exact activity code, eligibility, turnover cap, invoice format, tax treatment, foreign-currency receipt and export rules. It may be unsuitable for an activity or income level outside the regime.

### 6.5 Required written checks before accepting money

Ask the bank and a qualified local tax/legal adviser, using the actual funder/client agreement, to confirm:
- whether the payment is a grant, prize, bounty, reimbursement or service-export receipt;
- whether a resident individual may receive it in the proposed personal/professional status;
- the correct account and supporting documents;
- declarations, repatriation deadlines, permitted currency use, bank fees and timing;
- invoice/contract/tax requirements, where applicable;
- treatment of project budgets and reimbursed expenses;
- any foreign payer, intermediary, platform, sanctions or KYC restrictions.

**No registration, bank account, tax status or external payment arrangement has been created or changed by this research.**

### 6.6 Lawful constraint-solving

When a provider or government rule blocks a path, identify the precise activity, actor, jurisdiction, payment class and rule. Then evaluate lawful alternatives: direct individual eligibility, an eligible professional regime, a genuine fiscal host/sponsor, a licensed partner or subcontractor, an open/interoperable architecture, local execution, different provider, data minimisation or a different market.

Never use false residence, borrowed accounts, false invoices, hidden beneficial ownership, sanctions/KYC evasion, fictitious activity descriptions or an unlicensed regulated role.
