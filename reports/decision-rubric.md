# Decision Rubric

Strategic decisions: KEEP, MERGE, EXTRACT, FREEZE, ARCHIVE, UNKNOWN.

- KEEP: independent continuation is justified.
- MERGE: another repository should become the canonical home.
- EXTRACT: preserve selected components, ideas, or evidence elsewhere.
- FREEZE: preserve history/research without active implementation.
- ARCHIVE: no justified current investment after adequate inspection.
- UNKNOWN: evidence is insufficient.

Deep assessment dimensions are rated 0-5:
technical substance, validation/evidence, strategic value, reuse potential, completion cost.

Scores guide decisions; they do not replace evidence or judgment.
